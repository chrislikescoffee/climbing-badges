# Safe Climbing Club Badges

A digital recognition system for climbing club members to track skill requirements, log field evidence, earn badges, and nominate peers.

## Language

**Badge**:
A structured achievement awarded to a member upon verifying criteria or committee vote.
_Avoid_: Achievement, trophy, sticker, patch

**Requirement**:
A distinct, measurable criterion required to earn or advance a badge.
_Avoid_: Criterion, task, sub-goal, rule

**Progression**:
The current state of a member's progress toward completing a badge's requirements and achieving award status.
_Avoid_: User progress, tracking, state

**Award Cycle**:
The completion transition of all requirements for a badge, which awards the badge and prepares repeatable or upgradable badges for the next iteration.
_Avoid_: Level up, reset, recurrence

**Repeatable Badge**:
A badge that can be earned multiple times, tracking an incremental awarded count.
_Avoid_: Multi-badge, recurring badge

**Upgradable Badge**:
A badge with sequential visual tiers (e.g. Bronze, Silver, Gold) earned through successive award cycles.
_Avoid_: Tiered badge, leveled badge

**Evidence Log**:
An immutable record of progress submitted toward a requirement, containing count delta, notes, and photos.
_Avoid_: Proof, submission, audit log

**Nomination**:
A peer proposal recommending a member for a badge, subject to committee review and voting threshold.
_Avoid_: Recommendation, endorsement, referral

**Approval Mode**:
The validation policy governing whether a badge is self-awarded or requires committee vote.
_Avoid_: Verification level, review type

**Activity**:
An immutable event record documenting a notable club milestone, such as a member joining, a badge created or updated, or an award cycle completed.
_Avoid_: Event, log entry, notification, feed item
